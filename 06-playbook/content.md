# The content system

Content is the sales channel. There is no ad budget, so the daily output is the demand
generation. This is the operating shape, not a suggestion.

## Cadence

| Where | How often | What |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | one a day | 9–23 minute talking-head explainer with motion graphics carrying the data |
| X, personal | four to five a day | posts, threads, replies under decision-makers |
| X, business | three a day | the company's own voice on the same topics |
| LinkedIn | later | only when a channel is actually needed; weigh it then |

## The format

Shot on a phone, audio straight from the phone. No studio, no office, no script read from a
screen. The talking head moves around the frame while motion graphics carry the numbers
beside him. The design system — type, colour, where the head sits, how a number animates, what
never happens — is defined once and reused, so every video looks like the same product.

Anti-rules matter as much as rules. The thing that makes the style feel professional is the
list of things it never does.

## The writing

Every video and post goes three to six logic passes deep before a line is written. The first
layer is the news. The third layer is why it happened. The fifth is what it will cause that
nobody is pricing yet. That depth is the only defensible difference between this channel and
every other account covering the same continent.

## The reply game

Follow the decision-makers, not the commentators. Reply on their posts with something that
adds a layer they did not have, and never with a pitch. The profile does the selling: a
stranger clicks the face, reads the pinned post, and understands the position in five seconds.

## The pinned post

One post, pinned forever, that states what the channel is for and what it publishes. It is the
front door for a minister, a permanent secretary or a CEO who arrived from a reply.

## Brand parity

Same name, same portrait, same handle, same colours on X and YouTube. The business account is
the company's voice; the personal account is his. They should look like family, not twins.

## Volume as insurance

The plan is to bank far more material than the calendar needs — hundreds of videos and copy
blocks — so that a bad week, a bad month, or a slow sales cycle never breaks the rhythm. The
archive is what makes consistency possible without motivation.
