## How to give useful help from the web

1. Name the file and line you are answering, and the picture number if it is about a look.
2. Say what is wrong, show it, give one fix. Not five options.
3. A number carries its source and date, or it is marked estimated.
4. Do not redesign what Ambi approved. He said on 24 Nov: "No different directions, just making things better and better."
5. End with the one thing that should be tested first.
