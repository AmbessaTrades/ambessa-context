# 02 — The software

Working name: none. It is not "TubeLoop 2.0"; it is the software, and it will be named when it earns one.

## The one thing it must do

**Take a channel pack and produce a finished video that channel's audience would have watched anyway** —
paced, packaged and titled the way that channel already proved, without a human touching an editor.

## What a channel pack is

Niche, spine, design systems (as separable layers), plate classes, script system, schedule, title and
thumbnail grammar, and the channel's nine measurements. Three channels are three packs. A pack is the
unit of work, the unit of scale and the unit of billing.

## What "almost nothing to open a channel" means

Channel eleven is a clone: same pack version, a new identity and a new subject list. If opening a
channel needs code, it is not scale — it is a project. This is the only reason the marginal-cost claim
in the business exists at all.

## Hard constraints

- **Rust for at least 80% of the code.** The app, the engine and the phone client share one core.
- **One core, three surfaces:** desktop, phone, and a job runner that keeps working when the laptop is
  shut.
- **Renders inside six hours** where a heavier format is asked for; nothing that cannot be cut by code.
- **No counting.** Simplification is a sentence about how a film should feel, never a budget of objects.
- **Every film is art.** The test is where the viewer's mind goes, not how efficiently the pipeline ran.
