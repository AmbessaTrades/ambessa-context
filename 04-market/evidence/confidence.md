# Evidence and confidence

Distinguish an idea, a founder decision, a source-backed interpretation and a verified implementation.

## What each label proves

- **Founder decision:** a direct instruction with its source and date. Preserve exact wording where it matters.
- **Overview:** an authored interpretation of linked material. Follow the links for full nuance.
- **Plan or recipe:** describes intended behavior or the instructions used by an engine. It does not prove a successful run.
- **Logged observation:** someone recorded this at that time. It may be partial or wrong.
- **Runtime verification:** the named destination was exercised with the named inputs and result.
- **Reference extraction:** preserve its evidence depth: chapter close read, synthesis, summary, fan reception or proposal. Do not flatten them into the same certainty.
