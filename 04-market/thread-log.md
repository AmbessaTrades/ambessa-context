## What the evidence says (research, 8 Nov; ~350 sources)

- **Observation:** Africa and the Caribbean want AI, but the money is mostly on paper (Kenya's KSh 152B AI figure is a requirement, not cash; Microsoft/G42's $1B Kenyan data centre stalled).
- **Observation:** "Nobody local and trusted can deliver" is FALSE. Kenyan firms run eCitizen, Rwanda's Irembo won the 2026 GovTech prize, the big four consultancies are in the Caribbean, Estonia holds the trusted-outsider seat, and Gulf AI (G42/Presight) signed in 9+ African countries.
- **Observation:** The real gap is delivery: strategies that never become working systems, banks stuck at pilots, and small and mid-size firms with no simple, priced AI offer.
- **Observation:** Swahili AI is not a moat (big tech gives it away). Kikamba and noisy calls mixing Swahili, Sheng and English are open.
- **Observation:** Government is a moat but takes 12–24 months, and Kenya owes suppliers ~KSh 466–525B. Ways in: donor projects, subcontracting.
- **Observation:** The Africa–Caribbean link is a 10–15 year bet: trade is ~$729M/yr (ceiling ~$1.65B by 2030), with one scheduled flight (Air Peace Lagos–Antigua–Barbados, twice a month). Afreximbank has a $5B Caribbean line. Diaspora money is bigger: Kenya received $5.04B and Jamaica $3.49B in remittances in 2025.
- **Observation:** A general "AI employee" is a commodity (xAI Grok Bot, OpenAI Dots, Meta Muse, all Aug–Sep 2026). What works is one narrow job with a number (Avoca, ~$1B, phone agents for HVAC and plumbing). $25K + $3K/mo only fits firms with 20–200 staff. AI sales-rep products churn 70–80%.
- **Observation:** Hormozi puts "AI implementation" in the expert-dependent tier: rich on income, hard to sell. Billion-scale needs a moat or a supply/demand window. Uncool, regulated businesses (collections, inspections, funerals, elder care) have fewer rivals.
- **Observation:** Guyana is short 100–160K workers (grew 43.8% in 2024). Jamaica's BPO sector fell from 60K to 40K jobs. Kenyan bank bad loans hit 17.6% in 2025, a 20-year high.

## Held thesis (Mungura's proposal, not yet decided)

**An AI front desk for boring businesses that lose money on missed calls and slow replies.** It answers every call, WhatsApp and email, books the job and chases unpaid invoices. One result: "you stop losing customers." Sold online from Malindi. Buyers are picked by pain signals (reviews saying "never picks up", receptionist job posts, busy places with dead websites), not by community. The way in is Bruce's method: build their front desk first, then call their own line as the demo. Each deal is priced as a slice of the value recovered. The long game: case studies become Ambi's story on X and YouTube, the front desk grows into the back office, and the network grows toward the Afribbean link.

Superseded leans (kept for history): SACCO arrears recovery as the lead (Ambi unsure; it is now a feature), lead software (Ambi: crowded), and moving the market to Guyana or Jamaica (Ambi: his roots are not his market).
