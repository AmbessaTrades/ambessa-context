WHAT
Juju is private software, Rust first, on a Mac app, a web app and an Android APK. A channel profile (a Vessel) goes in; finished faceless
YouTube films come out, 52-minute TV-style at best, in any niche, from any pasted video, in our own way. Two people use it. The build began on 24 November 2026.
