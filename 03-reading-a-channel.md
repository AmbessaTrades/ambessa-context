# 03 — Reading a channel

A channel is read the same way every time, so two channels can be compared.

## Nine measurements

Seven frames at fixed fractions of the runtime (2, 18, 35, 52, 68, 84, 95%) plus the transcript:

1. **Plate dwell** — seconds between visible changes, median and range.
2. **Cuts per minute.**
3. **Caption rate** — words on screen per minute.
4. **Caption length** — words per line, lines per card.
5. **Text presence** — share of runtime carrying any text.
6. **Chapter cadence** — seconds between section cards.
7. **Speech rate** — words per minute of narration.
8. **Plate classes** — the mix of painting, photo, 3D, map, diagram, flat illustration.
9. **Colour drift** — the palette at each sample, and how it transitions.

Two channels are the same format only when all nine are within tolerance.

## The four formats read so far

- **Archive-plate documentary** (Bygone Philosophy): paintings, engravings, photographed book pages with
  the quoted lines underlined, a numeral on black, a name card on black. Captions bottom-left in small
  serif, slow pans.
- **Level-card walkthrough** (Jade Rabbit Hole): flat vector illustration, a chapter card carrying one
  subtitle line, a recurring number on screen. The viewer counts levels.
- **Mixed-plate archaeology** (ExtinctZoo): archival photograph, film-strip title card, 3D render in
  teal, artefact on white, cave-art montage. No captions. The variety of surface is the design.
- **Rendered cosmos** (The Opening, Universe Dimensions): one visual class at high finish, long holds,
  a diagram beat every few minutes.

## Reading the library images

The library holds contact sheets — one file, four to eight frames. Upscale before describing; crop the
frame out rather than squinting at the sheet; never quote a number that is illegible at the resolution
you have.
