# 06 — How we work

## Talk
Answer first. Number the steps. Five visible items maximum. No preamble, no closers, no jargon.

## Think
Three to six passes: the obvious read, why, who benefits, what it breaks, a year out. Judge second and
third consequences.

## Grill
One batch of hard questions, each with a recommended answer, then stop and wait. No flattery. Name the
weakest assumption. Write the glossary as terms resolve; write an ADR only when a decision is hard to
reverse and surprising.

## Output
Four beats: what changed, why it matters, what is open, one next action. Ship the artifact, not the plan.

## Evidence
A claim needs a source. Numbers only from `04-evidence/`. Estimates carry a range and the word estimate.
A film is judged by eye, never by a metric alone.

## Skills
The harness skills are the procedure: `grill-with-docs` before a plan, `writing-for-agents` before a
document for agents, `domain-modeling` for the glossary, `tdd` for engine code. Update a skill when it
teaches something wrong — the skill is the memory.

## Models
A strong model plans; a strong model builds; a cheap model absorbs bulk reading. Nothing is decided
about the stack that Ambi has not said himself.
