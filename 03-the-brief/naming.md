# Naming the venture — what is being asked for, and everything tried

## What he is asking for

A name that is **inspiring** — not clever, not tasteful, not a smart piece of etymology. It has to feel like a world you want to be inside, the way Palantir feels like a seeing-stone from a story with gods in it, and Anthropic feels like an institution even though nobody had used the word before. It has to be **good on the tongue**: short enough to say once, hard enough to remember, easy enough that nobody asks you to repeat it. It should represent **weaving** — of cultures, of markets, of people, of fact into account — without using the word weaver. And it should work in the mouths of a Nairobi buyer, a Kingston buyer, a Toronto investor and a Washington procurement officer at the same time.

One more quality, stated by him directly: it should carry a **function inside a cosmology**, not a description of a service. A role, an instrument, or a station in an order of things. A word with a job.

## How particular he is about this

He rejects a name on sound alone, and one syllable is enough to kill it — "weaver" is out not for its meaning but because it sits too close to a word he cannot unhear. He has read 2,882 chapters of *Shadow Slave* and the bar is that feeling, not a dictionary entry, which is why every obscure-but-correct word offered so far has read as trying too hard. He dislikes deity names because they arrive as Pan-African branding to buyers who are not Pan-African, and he dislikes invented compounds because they sound like startups. He will not be argued into a name. It has to land the first time he says it out loud.

## The hard constraints

1. Inspiring, and good spoken aloud. No diacritics on a business card.
2. Implies weaving, joining, fate or linkage — without the word weaver.
3. Reads as an institution to a $10M–$5B buyer, and not as a flag to anyone else.
4. Not a deity. Not a defeat. Not an invented prefix.
5. Not already owned in defence, software, or treasury.
6. Two layers are allowed: the firm legible outside, the internal vocabulary mythic.

## Where this stands

Nothing is chosen. No name in this file has been accepted, and the ones marked rejected were rejected on sound, on ownership, or on what they evoke — never on meaning. The working hypothesis, in his words: weaving the strings of fate is probably the thing the company does, so the name is more likely to be a role inside a cosmology than a word about documents. Nothing here should be treated as a recommendation.
