# The money model

## Who pays

Two buyers, both high ticket:

1. **Governments** in Africa and the Caribbean — ministries, investment promotion agencies,
   procurement authorities, audit offices, state-owned banks.
2. **Companies doing $10M to $5B** — exporters, importers, banks, logistics firms,
   manufacturers, and diaspora-owned businesses trading into these markets.

Not consumers. Not small businesses. Not subscriptions at $30 a month.

## What it costs

| Structure | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding, government | $15k–$25k | one engagement, one deliverable, one country or one deal |
| Onboarding, company | $10k–$15k | same, smaller scope |
| Retainer while live | $3k–$5k a month | the file stays current, the questions keep getting answered |
| Verification dossier alone | $10k | for a buyer not ready for the room |
| Standing register (agencies) | $60k–$150k a year | every bidder, every tender, refreshed |

The floor is $5k. Nothing below that is worth answering the phone for.

## What the first 60 days look like

- Content runs daily from day one — it costs time, not money, and it is the only channel that
  works before there is a reference client.
- First paying client is the target inside 60 days, from the first 20 conversations, not from
  the first 200 posts.
- The build sprint is 23 November. Anything shipped must answer one question: how does this
  make money now.

## What money is being spent on

Model tokens and API calls, cheaply, and deliberately. Where a job can be done by a cheaper
model, it is. The expensive model is kept for judgement and for the writing that carries the
brand.

## The multiplier that matters

Five clients at $15k onboarding plus $5k a month is roughly $100k in the first year from one
service line, with no investor. That is the shape being built. Everything else — sponsorship,
courses, a larger software product — sits behind that and is not allowed to delay it.
