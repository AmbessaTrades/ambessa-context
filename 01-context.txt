THE FIVE WALLS (numbers verified in 02-findings.csv)
1. Payment rails. Caribbean banks lost about 4 in 10 correspondent relationships in 15 years. No
   local-currency settlement between CARICOM and Africa. Every African letter of credit is
   confirmed by a bank outside the region. PAPSS is live in 30+ countries but Nigeria settled only
   N5.6bn (~$3.6m) in H1 2025, down 53%.
2. Trade finance. Africa's gap is $74bn. Bank intermediation fell to 23% of African merchandise
   trade against ~80% globally. West Africa: $14bn gap, 21% of applications rejected.
3. Logistics. 1,600 miles apart with no direct link. Caribbean importers pay 12-21% of goods
   value in transport and insurance. Cargo sits ~3 weeks in African ports; Lagos over 20 days.
4. State behaviour. Kenya: Sh511.75bn verified pending bills. Zambia and Ghana pay late as a
   budgeting habit. Namibia cannot enforce a foreign arbitral award without a certificate.
5. The unpublished gate. Caribbean SPS approval has no published risk assessment for most
   products. CARICOM processed 192 tariff suspension requests. Rum faces an 88% tariff in African
   markets. Africa-Caribbean trade has never exceeded 6% of bilateral exports.

THE MARKET
East Africa is politically engaged with the Caribbean and commercially absent. China buys the
tender before it opens: over $2bn of financed surveillance across 11 countries, the vendor named
inside the loan. India wins by being the non-Chinese option: Ghana bought a >$200m 5G stack to
avoid Chinese vendors, NPCI licensed UPI to Namibia. The corridor is live but unserved: WapiPay is
the only startup with a licensed live Africa-Caribbean corridor; Zeepay's Barbados arm was
suspended by the central bank. Nobody operates the trusted middle for one mid-market deal.

HOW TO ANSWER
Answer first. Number steps. Five items maximum. No preamble, no closers, no jargon. Run three to
six logic passes before writing. Judge second and third consequences, not the first. Perception
beats reality: a buyer judges his own risk, not the product. People move for money, status,
promotion or entertainment - name which. A claim needs a source and a page, a label when it
cannot be checked, and a split between what is said, what is inferred and what is unknown.
