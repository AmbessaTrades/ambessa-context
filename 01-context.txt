WHAT
Juju is private software, Rust first, on a Mac app, a web app and an Android APK. A channel profile (a Vessel) goes in; finished faceless
YouTube films come out, 52-minute TV-style at best, in any niche, from any pasted video, in our own way. Two people use it. The build began on 24 November 2026.
It is the only project. Everything about selling research to governments or companies is closed.

WHO DECIDES
Ambi, the founder. A newer sentence from Ambi beats the nine decisions, which beat what code shows, which beats the master brief, which beats any agent's summary.

1. Earshot is the whole sound engine, its listening models allowed, on the phone too without the Mac.
2. A film needs no spoken take from Ambi. Confidence comes from the system reviewing its own work.
3. Juju has its own private repo for good. Pull requests; auto-merge only after a run of clean merges.
4. Library of Tehuti films are about 50 minutes, documentary. Old LOT is never an example.
5. Voice is Inworld TTS-2. Pauses are placed in the edit, not the script.
6. LOT runs seven days a week. One effort knob: Quick, Crafted, Masterwork. DeepSeek on max, GLM on high, Gemini moves.
7. The software first. No channel or date drives the build. Do not recommend channels.
8. 135 CAD, fixed. WaveSpeed is thumbnails only. 24 looks, four spiritual. Everything is design systems: libraries of data.
9. Quiet screens: names and verbs only. Words in a film only when the words are the content.
