# Explored and rejected

Eleven idea rounds have already been run and killed. Ambi's verdict on the session that produced
them: *"it fell short in a lot of places."* A new agent that re-proposes one of these without new
evidence is wasting his time.

Nothing here is a decision or a favourite. It is a map of dead ground.

## The five ways an idea died

An idea has to survive all five:

1. **The government builds it itself** — no external buyer exists.
2. **Paperwork was never the real blocker** — payment, authority or politics was.
3. **No fear with a deadline** — nothing bad happens if the buyer does nothing this month.
4. **The market expires** — a rule or a regime change ends the need.
5. **The buyer will not trust an unknown seller** — no named penalty, no finished sample, no peer
   who already bought.

No idea so far has all three of: a named penalty, a finished sample, and a buyer saying yes.

## Numbers from that session, and how far to trust each

- **Kenya bills:** 115,617 claims worth KSh664.8bn reviewed, KSh155.34bn recommended; cabinet
  approved KSh23.74bn for 28,726 small bills (peopledaily.digital). The follow-on arithmetic —
  531 larger bills, about KSh131.6bn — is that agent's calculation, **not published**.
- **Kenya totals conflict by date:** the evidence base holds Sh511.75bn (March 2025); other
  sources give Sh528.36bn and Sh571bn. Always date the figure.
- **Guyana:** local-content fines of GY$1m–GY$50m per offence exist; **no named company was found
  fined**, so a fine is a threat, not a demonstrated event.
- **Guyana fronting pattern:** 51% on paper, few assets, capital advanced as a loan (cijn.org).
- **EU forest rule:** applies from 30 December 2026; the €40,000-a-year figure is one exporter's
  claim relayed by a vendor, not a market rate.
- **Evidence-base counts:** that session claimed this repo inflated 214/204 and counted 199/189.
  Checked directly: the database holds **214 rows, 204 verified**, and the rendered page shows
  204 verified cards, 10 unconfirmed hidden, 26 deal-blockers. The repo is right.

## Errors that session admitted

Called the Kenya approval rate "about 25%" when it is about 23%. Said the EU deadline was 44 days
away when it was 43. Never confirmed the "4% of turnover" penalty or the June 2027 small-firm
date. Moved one idea's score 24 → 23 → 21 on judgement alone. Could not scan X, run sub-agents or
update this repo at all.

## What it means for the next agent

- Do not lead with one artefact. Produce a ranked set and let him pick.
- Do not open with Kenya, and do not open with anything a ministry could build itself.
- A named buyer and a named penalty beat a clever mechanism.
- Say what happens if he does nothing. If nothing happens, it is not a purchase.
- The evidence base is verified; use it instead of re-deriving facts.
