# 05 — The record

What shipped, what earned, what failed. Dated.

## Earned

Library of Tehuti: 126,892 views and **$1,031.50** at **$8.13** per thousand (YouTube Analytics, 2026). 277,000 subscribers, 3,700 median views.
No upload since 2 November 2026. Juju has earned nothing: it has made no film.

## Lessons carried in

1. **A pipeline is not a product.** The old app reached a working one-file flow and published nothing with it.
2. **One subject until it dies.** Thirty-seven films on one subject: about $15 a day, then a peak of $106, then $27, with pay per thousand flat.
3. **Green gates over empty pages.** A critic that counts and never looks passes an empty page.
4. Twenty-two builds were audited; one reached strangers.

## What broke on the way (the agents' own list)

- The stage test gave the fillers a longer script than the engine had; 10 to 11 scenes were dropped.
- The first commit swept in about 370 MB of museum pictures and the push hung. The pictures stay in the private history.
- Five budget numbers in one morning, all from assumptions. The fix was Ambi's fixed sum and a ledger that meters every call.
- "All checks green" was said too early; corrected at 13:21 (above).
- A note to a teardown agent started a second copy of it; the copy was stopped.
