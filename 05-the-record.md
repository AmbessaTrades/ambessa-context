# 05 — The record

What shipped, what earned, what failed. Dated. Numbers only where the source is dated.

## What earned

**LOT** — 126,892 views and **$1,031.50** at **$8.13** per thousand views (YouTube Analytics, 2026).
That is the only money this business has taken from content so far.

## What shipped and did not earn

Twenty-two projects across two years produced an artefact; one produced revenue. The pattern is not a
lack of building — it is building without a channel that had a buyer.

## Three failure patterns, named

1. **A pipeline is not a product.** Loop reached a working one-file flow and never published a film with
   it; the software was judged by its architecture, not by a video someone watched.
2. **Repeating one format until it dies.** Thirty-seven LOT videos on one subject took a channel from
   about $15 a day to $106 peak and then to $27, while pay per thousand views held flat. The audience
   left because the subject stopped moving, not because the edit got worse.
3. **Green gates over empty pages.** A critic that counts and never looks passes an empty page. Every
   numeric gate needs an eye on the artefact.

## Why this project is different, if it is

It is the first one whose product is the thing that makes the channels, and whose first two customers
are the two people who will use it that week.
