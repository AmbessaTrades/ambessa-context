## Where review happens

1. **Code**: `cargo test`, skills valid, `shot.py` (layout and quiet faults), `truth.py` (nothing unreal on screen), `gate.sh` for all of it, GitHub Actions on every pull request.
2. **Fresh eyes**: a critic who has read nothing looks at the picture; another presses every button by script.
3. **Attackers**: forgers plant false facts, skeptics break the plan, a hunter looks for false alarms.
4. **Blind tests**: ours beside theirs, names masked.

Weak spot, stated by the critics: in the conductor a blind critic that finds nothing still lets a film pass. Open.
