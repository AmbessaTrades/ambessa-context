# ADR 0026: the pots are for sixty days of films; Juju learns kinds of story; DeepSeek writes at its deepest thinking

Working document, changes with conversation. Decided 25 Nov 2026, about 23:10 (Ambi, second round of the grilling). Quotes are Ambi's; what is open is said so.

1. **The pots.** "each of these api stack pots left are designed for 60 days worth of videos, not for testing ... leeway with an extra $70 CAD max we can add, so we cant go over that ... (maybe another $70 after 30 days ...)". Read 25 Nov 23:12: Inworld 38.24 US (Ambi), DeepSeek 12.97 (live), WaveSpeed 11.71 (live), OpenRouter 6.89 (live), Google AI Studio 9.37 CAD (Ambi). The test cap is 35.00 US, "most of it is DeepSeek".
2. **Zero-shot.** Juju is taught kinds of story (five named: something breaks in a chain; water rises or falls; a border or empire moves; a route is cut and prices change; a thing spreads town to town); six or seven are welcome "so it's very flexible". Every seat of the stack is taught each kind.
3. **Who does the work.** The whole film must be within DeepSeek's and GLM 5.3's reach; Neon may govern through the tests. Opus may build the system, never make the films.
4. **Thinking.** "Writing can't run with no thinking": writing, planning and any creative preparation by DeepSeek runs at its deepest level ("extra high"; DeepSeek serves low, high and max; max is used, with room for it). Second, third and fourth order thinking (ripple-sight, nyansapo-ladder, the story law) belongs in every creative seat.
5. **Juju Poly** means the library of assets (Juju Poly assets), one of the Juju Powers; not a polygon ground. Its use on the Night film "looked really good ... fit everything very well". Clears ADR 0025.
6. **The money must be seen:** live balances in the software and a tiny window on the Mac ("so i don't have to keep opening a brave browser"), after the more pressing work.

Open: films in sixty days and their length; what Neon keeps; where the extra money goes; the voice model; saving the maps engine to the private GitHub (asked, not answered).
