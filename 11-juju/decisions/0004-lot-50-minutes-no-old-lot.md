# 0004. LOT films are 50 minute TV-style documentaries, and old LOT is not a source

> **25 Nov 2026:** for map films, length is about 30 minutes (ADR 0022 item 6). This stays the record for LOT.

**Status:** decided by Ambi, 24 Nov 2026: "LOT honestly has been 50 minute videos, period. Those TV style documentary stuff. But now in the style of Pursuit of Wonder, or [...] we will have some options that match the style we need." And: "Do not take any examples from LOT before. LOT videos before are trash. Our master MD has all the reasons."

**Supersedes.** The brief's 13 to 17 minutes for LOT (16.2), the 10 Nov road format, and every use of `lot-r01` in this repo (the 85 second proof, the stage test, the room samples).
