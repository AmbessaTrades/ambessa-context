# 0005. Voice is TTS-2, and pauses are placed in the edit

**Status:** decided by Ambi, 24 Nov 2026: "TTS-2, done. [...] if something needs that one second, two second pause, we do not need to do that within the script writing itself any more; we can do that while it is editing its own timeline, that is headless."

**Decision.** Juju voices use Inworld TTS-2. The script carries no pause marks. The headless edit step places silences (after heavy numbers, at turns, around the product moment) on the timeline, where they can be changed without paying for voice again.
