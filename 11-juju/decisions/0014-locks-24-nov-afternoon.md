# 0014 Locks of 24 Nov, afternoon

> Working document. Subject to change through continued conversation. Nothing here is a requirement until Ambi says so.

**Status:** accepted (Ambi, 24 Nov 2026, about 14:30 EAT: "Lock these decisions and visual fixes in right now"). Binding for every worker. Where an older file differs, this one wins.

1. **Runtime and money.** Daily baseline: 20 minutes, at most 2,400 spoken words (about 18 to 20 minutes with real pauses), three channels a day, two voiced and one silent, so the voice wallet stays above zero with its reserve through day 60. The 50 minute Masterwork stays in the engine as an option, not the default. Supersedes the 50 minute default of 0004 and the daily effort of 0006 for now.
2. **Vendor names** never appear on an app screen: Tongue, Pigment, Elder, Griot, Scribe, Watcher only. They live behind the key sheet and in internal files.
4. **No narrator subtitle.** A film never prints the sentence being spoken at the bottom of the frame. Only a look that is openly silent-cinema may caption.
5. **Split zones.** Type never sits on a busy face, page, crowd or painting detail. The picture is feathered to one side; type has its own plain high contrast ground (solid, navy or cream) on the other, with 40 percent or more of the frame empty. Allowed and wanted: one loaded word alone; a heavy count-up on deep navy; a two-bar chart; a title with one line. No chat bubbles over faces.
6. **Grounds and type roles.** No universal brown. Ink-navy (near #080C1C), charcoal, cream parchment. Monospace for machine words and transcripts; heavy geometric sans for numbers, chapters and quotes; high contrast serif for a literary word.
7. **Maps** run full bleed or feather into dark. No straight cut edge, no picture edge showing.
8. **Agreement in code.** A counter, date or number on screen equals the token being spoken at that moment, or the film fails. Any two text boxes that overlap fail the frame.
9. **Certainty over tricks.** Flat planes drawn perfectly. Fragile fake 2.5D layering is out: no parallax cut-outs, no light beams, dust or sparks pasted over paintings, no half-melted dissolves. Film grain and grade only where they hide nothing and help.
10. **The cheap model's job** is a tiny three-step multiple choice per stretch (which layout; which of three pictures already judged fit; which word the change lands on), with a target of 95 percent legal at first answer.
11. **Timing laws** (measured 24 Nov): the picture leads its naming noun by about 2.5 s (measured median 2.7 s early, 33 of 41 cases, picked by eye); about 1.3 s of silence before a story turn (measured medians 0.62 to 1.28 s by film); about 3.0 s at a chapter (2.86 and 3.38 s); a hold after a number only on the one or two numbers that carry the film.
12. **People** are shown only as real historical paintings, portraits, statues, photographs or footage. None available: use a quotation, map, number or timeline. Never a stand-in prop.
13. **The bar.** Two looks: (A) dark navy and cream essay, learned from ZgpMtombn9A; (B) crisp telemetry and map, learned from Milky Stellar and Atticus King. The mixed blind sheet is run on stills BEFORE any voice is bought, in maker, blind judge, fixer loops, and the films are brought to Ambi only when the blind judges stop catching our frames. Voice is bought once and cached. Half-baked drafts are not shown.

Lead's reading of "stop catching": over a fresh sheet of 12 of ours and 12 published, three judges who never saw an earlier round call ours machine-made no more often than they call the published frames machine-made plus one frame in ten. If loops stop improving, the lead says so plainly rather than lower the bar.

14. **One hour from the tap to a finished film, any length; ninety minutes is the hard stop.** Measured 24 Nov, Mac at
    load 13: the engine draws 195 s of film in 387 s (1.98 s for each second of film). At that speed a 20 minute film
    renders in 40 minutes (fits, barely) and a 50 minute film in 99 minutes (fails). Target: at most 1.0 s for each
    second of film on this Mac, proven by a timed run, before a 50 minute film is offered.
15. **Nothing is lost by closing the app.** Every stage writes hashed files; reopening shows the true stage and time left.
16. **No dead rooms or controls.** A control is backed by something that runs today, or it is removed. At most two
    "soon" marks in the whole app.

    inside one hour cannot wait for a batch with a 24 hour target. Default becomes: ask for batch, and if it is not back
