# 0002. A film needs no spoken take from Ambi

**Status:** decided by Ambi, 24 Nov 2026, in conversation: "It can still be done without me verbally saying something for two minutes [...] VidRush V2 is something, right? You're not verbally saying stuff."

**Context.** YouTube's monetisation policy (read on its help page, 24 Nov 2026) excludes AI content made with generic templates "without adding the creator's original, authentic insights or perspective". The lead proposed a required two minute spoken take per film. Ambi refused it.

**Decision.** No per-film human input is required. Confidence comes from the system reviewing its own work: checks, fresh reviewers, fixes, and rules that grow from every defect, in the build fleet and inside the app.

**Consequences.** *(Lead's inference, not said:)* the channel's point of view has to live in the channel pack, written once when the channel is opened (its stance, its sources, what it refuses to say), so every film carries a perspective without a daily chore. An optional one-line steer stays in the composer. Whether a pack-level stance is enough for the policy is unproven; the first films will show it.
