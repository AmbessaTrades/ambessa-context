# ADR 0024: every move is heard, quietly, and a channel owns its sounds

Working document, changes with conversation. Decided 25 Nov 2026, about 21:40 (Ambi, watching the Istanbul film): "every little element of it can just subtle it can be quiet but it has to be there"; "not everything is like ... water sound effect is playing literally all throughout the thing"; "when the camera is moving rapidly ... subtle whooshes that are timed to the exact second or milliseconds that it's taken for the camera actual motion"; numbers that count up "should have like some cool little" jingle; "sound effect packs that it assigns to a particular channel that you can accept or swap around, not too much ... part of your brand profile with your color palettes"; "a DeepSeek ... low thinking can just find out every single part ... for all the pockets".

1. Whatever moves, appears, counts, draws, fills, opens, closes or leaves on the picture has a sound of its own, small and under the voice, at its own moment and as long as its own motion. Supersedes the line in `stack/cards/sound.md` that made lines, lands, names and landings silent and forbade whooshes.
2. A camera move is heard for exactly as long as it moves, and swells with its real speed.
3. Real recorded things (a pump, a quake, magma) stay, louder than the small ones. Place ambience (water, wind) comes and goes; it never runs the whole film.
4. A channel owns a sound pack, next to its colours, in its profile: proposed by Juju, accepted or swapped sound by sound, not too many.
5. DeepSeek, thinking low, goes over the film pocket by pocket and chooses from the pack; code owns the time and the loudness.
6. Picture and sound read one timeline written by the engine itself, so they cannot drift. (Recommended by the lead, not said by Ambi in these words.)

Kept from before: nothing that sounds like an 8-bit game, no boom, no riser for show.
