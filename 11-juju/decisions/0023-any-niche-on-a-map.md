# ADR 0023: any niche a map can carry; two night-and-day systems first; under $1.50 a film

Working document, changes with conversation. Decided 25 Nov 2026, about 21:20 (Ambi, answering the first round of the grilling). Their words are quoted; what they did not settle is marked open.

1. **Nothing is refused.** "I don't think Juju should refuse anything ... if you were to choose a design system that may not be good for your particular ideation then it just won't work as well but I don't think it should be restricted to anything": ancient, modern and middle ages war, ten kinds of finance, GDP, politics, pan-Africanism, migration, faith. Juju may say a form fits badly and offer a better one. It never stops.
2. **We build a system of maps, not a niche.** "we're not building for a particular niche we're building for a design system of maps that can handle different types of niches". Water, land, borders and built things that change, with what they do to an economy, are the strength today. Supersedes ADR 0022 item 1.
3. **Map channels only, for now:** one for Ambi and one for her. The layers must be copyable later to a channel that uses no map (Library of Tehuti); no work on that now.
4. **Duppy Night and Duppy Day lead.** "we're just locking this"; Duppy Day to S first, agreed; the newest Night film (Istanbul) is the best serious film of the day. Dusk (low poly) is its own category and waits.
5. **Money:** "under 150": a 28 minute film under $1.50. "we don't want neon doing absolutely everything": Neon stays where it has been seen to be better, and DeepSeek ("thinking, or if it thinks too long ... medium") must carry more. A seat moves only after the same film is seen both ways. Limits may rise later if the system and the app work really well. Supersedes ADR 0021 item 4 (no strong model in a film).
6. **Pockets:** two minutes at a time, marked by Ambi, across different niches ("a little flexible"). "We could refine that."
7. **The wordless time map** (studied: umstrUXqmRg) is a niche and a design system of its own, and Juju must be able to make it: "it's ideation ... our software has to be able to do this".

Open (recommended by the lead, not said by Ambi): which calls Neon keeps; 10 minutes, then 25, after the pockets. Open: the thesis rule (belief, its opposite, a thing that changes) as advice; Ambi asked for the question in plainer words.
