# ADR 0025: Juju Poly lives on every map

Working document, changes with conversation. Decided 25 Nov 2026, about 22:45 (Ambi): "the juju stack needs to learn to use poly on everything because it's always gonna be able to add an extra oomph, an extra beauty ... on top of whatever is being depicted ... matches whatever's going on and only adds to everything ... to all pockets, even for 20 minute plus videos ... it'll have to creatively think, that's another big part of the ideation and the storyboarding ... blend well with how our maps currently look ... customizing or tweaking a few things on the existing assets to fit exactly whatever mold is happening".

1. Low-poly bodies from the shelf are part of Duppy Night and Duppy Day, not only of Dusk. Amends ADR 0023 item 4 (Dusk as its own category stays; its bodies no longer do).
2. They act on the words and belong to the place; they never decorate (`juju-maps/stack/cards/bodies.md`).
3. Casting them is a seat of the stack (`juju-maps/bodies.py`), judged on its results and improved.
