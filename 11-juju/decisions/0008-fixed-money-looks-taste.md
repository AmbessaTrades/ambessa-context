# 0008. Money is fixed at 135 CAD; looks; where taste comes from

**Said by Ambi, 24 Nov 2026.**
- Money: "We have 135 Canadian [...] it falls into the budget. I am not adding anything. The software has to make the best of that, period." "WaveSpeed is only thumbnails." He judged the lead's earlier $290 US estimate "way off": too much for pictures, too little for the picture checker.
- Looks: "If there are twenty four ready made looks only four should be for spirituality", and their essence should "bleed into a channel profile if I were to just grab a video link and paste it in": the absorber sees which of the four a pasted spiritual video leans toward and carries that in. No look built on a count of pictures ("a 70 image essence [...] was a failing system").
- The Bygone Philosophy look (museum figures cut out on a dark ground) must be a ready-made look without pasting a video, "because we have done it already".
- Study "galactic empires don't actually make sense" (Atticus King, J0wWNytEXS4): "look how good the editing is [...] how can we make exactly that", listen to its audio with Earshot, its pacing, its paid promotion. "When I think maps I think boring sometimes": a map look must be as alive as that one.
- Taste comes from his YouTube account that has only ever clicked faceless channels (its feed and subscriptions). Never Shorts. Not always maps, not always group chats: look for the unfamiliar.
- On YouTube's policy: he was never struck; "we should not be afraid of that".

**Decision (the lead's split of the fixed 135 CAD, candidate until metered):** voice 55, picture checker 14, thumbnails 14, flagship scripts 21, daily scripts and checks 22, outlines 9. The app's tank meters every call and lowers effort before a wallet runs dry; it never asks for more money.

**Consequences.** In-film pictures come from free archives, code-drawn worlds and licensed footage, never from paid generation, until a wallet is added for it. The looks library is 24 with exactly four for spirituality and inner life.

**Added the same day (Ambi, 24 Nov):** "I agree with your exact mapping of the money." And: "The whole concept of everything is design systems. Develop design systems, develop skills that will be useful for you and for the software to extract what is needed to fit in those JSON templates [...] Our font library has got to be really, really unique, our colour palette libraries have got to be really, really unique, and the themings, how they map up with each other, and how we extract that off of a video [...] We have got to learn how to do quotes really well, different types of structures." "Make skills as you go."

**Consequence for every agent:** fonts, palettes, quote structures, motion devices and textures live as libraries of data in `design/` (see `docs/DESIGN-SYSTEMS.md` once written). A look, a channel profile and an absorbed video are all compositions of library entries plus the eight layers of brief 6.1. Nothing invents a one-off style outside the libraries; a new need becomes a new library entry.
