## How far to trust it
- Joins: ends good to about 2 frames (10 looked at). The survey calls fades what are dissolves and splits eight joins in two: read `cuts.json`. 8 joins the survey lacked were added, 11 double marks dropped.
- Events: 6 of 6 moments read off frame tiles before the pass (213.75, 431.35, 447.98, 454.63, 481.27, 49.88) have an event within 0.13 s. Rows on pictures are "weak": the push, not drawing. Four desk shots are "busy": their single slips are also listed. No other held-out proof.
- Painting and print names are by subject, artists not checked. Words are YouTube's automatic captions: Greek names are misheard by it at times. `act` is a colour rule.
- Survey zoom answers: scene names left generic ("next picture"); the names are in `cuts.json`.
