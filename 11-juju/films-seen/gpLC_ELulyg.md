## How far to trust it
- Survey's own cut list keeps 26 cuts; 1 is real and 2 sit on the fade. The answers "busy" did not take them out. My `cuts.json` is the one to read. A car through the lens fools every detector.
- Camera, pass, light and object rows are looked at on sheets (`exact` true where frame sheets bracket it; the rest within about 0.5 s). Object boxes are Estimated. Nearest word is the nearest caption: there is no voice.
- Ladder and goals before 146 s: the frame the line's right edge moved, only where that is clear; later within 1 s.
- Text: OCR with seven misreadings set right; "two hundred billion" caption while the OCR label reads million (299.3): label is billion by eye.
- The arrival map and frame difference over the whole film at 480 wide (Measured, 16 pieces of up to 40 s, 819 s): 1,912 raw rows, mostly the flight itself. They touch 46 of 119 caption entrances (the same shifted 2.1 s: 31), 7 of 40 readout lines (luck 7), 11 of 22 goals (luck 2). On a camera that never stops they cannot place the text; the overlay eye (`overlay.py`: pixels that stand still for 7 frames) does. This is a held out film and a real limit of SCORE.md's winner.
- `events.json` has the title twice (caption row Measured, title row Judged). Shots there are 40 s pieces, not cuts.
