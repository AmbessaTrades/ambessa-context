# Who Ambi is

31. Born in Canada to Jamaican-Guyanese parents, raised Pan-African, living in Kenya. His
parents are Caribbean; his home is now continental Africa. He is the bridge he is selling.

He thinks in long voice notes and speaks faster than he types. He does an hour of meditation
in the morning, studies with a guru in South India, and is learning Swahili. He came out of
finance and loves it. He has run several software attempts; the ones that worked were small,
and the ones that failed failed by growing features instead of customers.

**What he is building now:** a paid business and a personal brand at the same time. The brand
is Ambessa Saul — Africa-Caribbean trade, politics and economics, spoken plainly, daily, on
YouTube and X. The business sells high-ticket verification and deal work to governments and
companies. The content makes the market; the product takes the money.

**What he wants to be doing at 70:** still arguing about a coup in Mali, still flying to
Guyana, still saying our people should be trading with each other. This is not a niche test
for him; it is the thing he intends to be known for.

## How he talks, and how to talk back

- He corrects fast and expects the correction to stick. His words outrank any document.
- He does not want agreement. He wants the weak point named.
- He does not want to be told what he already decided. Present evidence, not instructions.
- No jargon. Not "price band per engagement" — "high ticket, $5k to $25k to onboard one
  client". Say the thing itself.
- He reads on a phone. Five items maximum, numbers before prose.

## What he believes about this market

- The two regions have never traded at any real scale, and every agreement is unimplemented.
  That absence is the opening, not the obstacle.
- Perception decides deals in government and enterprise. A better product loses to a better
  story, so the brand is not decoration; it is the sales channel.
- His face and his name are the only thing a competitor cannot buy. So nothing that hides him
  behind an avatar or a faceless brand will work.
- Authenticity beats polish. Phone audio, real passion, real documents, motion graphics that
  carry the data — not a script read from a screen.
- Family and personal ties exist in Guyana and the Caribbean at senior level. The details are
  held privately and are not for publication or for an agent to disclose.

## What he refuses

Consumer subscriptions. Cheap pricing. Copying someone else's funnel. Building features
before a buyer exists. Writing documentation nobody reads. Being told what he decided.
