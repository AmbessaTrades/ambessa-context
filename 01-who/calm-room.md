# Calm Room

Open each layer on the next decision and reveal detail only when it helps that decision.

## The event behind the model

On 11 October Ambi rejected a Brain map with 26 giant all-caps region names, colliding labels, dot clusters, dotted underlines, a minimap, counts and a bottom search bar. His complaint was how cluttered it felt and how little helped him decide. The binding correction outranks UX-V2 where they conflict. [[90 Sources/f163b5f7df7d4b81f1f795e8|Binding Calm Room correction, 11 October]]

## When to use it

Apply to every screen and every layer, including empty states and expanded tools. A home answers what to do now with at most three large cards, one sentence and one action each. Maps, lists and libraries are one click away; the landing screen is not the whole database.

## What must stay true

At rest, no more than 40 visible words excluding the left rail and at most seven visible data labels. Sentence case, short plain labels, no wide all caps or dotted underlines. Counts and IDs stay under Advanced. A number appears beside the sentence explaining why it matters. Shape, position, colour and motion carry relationships. Group into at most 5–7 clear tiles, each with its best three items.

Map explains relations, List compares candidates, Strata shows lineage and depth. Each view needs a distinct useful decision. Every element must help the founder choose or feel real progress.

## Failure modes and a recorded exception

Shrinking a wall of text does not remove clutter. Hiding the next action under a decorative graph defeats the model. Three equally noisy views do not become useful by having a switch. Calm is compatible with rich documents: depth belongs behind a click, not deleted from memory.

The 12 October integration log deliberately kept complete 2–7 word idea names where every three-word truncation became nonsense, with an explicit test exception. Preserve the binding rule and this dated exception together; do not elevate a three-word limit above understandable meaning.
