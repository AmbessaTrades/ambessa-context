The four production shapes

Not decisions — the space of what is possible, so next week's conversation starts inside it.

1. Real room, graphics in the room. No matte, no green. Cheapest, most human, hardest to make elegant. Graphics sit on walls, desks, maps.
2. Cut out into a designed world. Matte the subject, build the ground. Full control of colour and motion; every frame is a composition.
3. Face as anchor, world as b-roll. The camera never leaves him; the idea is shown, not designed.
4. Text as the image. The argument is the graphic. Fastest to produce, strongest on a phone screen, weakest at building warmth.
